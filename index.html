A bit about me

Most of what I do comes back to how we make consequential choices under uncertainty.

Right now I run Move 37 at Harvard's Belfer Center. We're a small team building, evaluating, and conceptualizing AI for statecraft. On the side I help funds and firms think about decision analysis and AI, along with a few of my own projects.

Before Harvard I spent a few years at the World Bank in Central Asia, working on policy and technology. Before that I studied History at Oxford and played professional rugby — which is where I learned about decisions under pressure.

Teaching

I am a teaching fellow for EC1415 / API-302: Analytic Frameworks for Policy5 at the Kennedy School — optimization, game theory, decision analysis, and interactive decision problems.

I've also designed and taught graduate classes on AI/ML, decision analysis, and economic complexity at the Universidad Católica Boliviana6 in La Paz.
